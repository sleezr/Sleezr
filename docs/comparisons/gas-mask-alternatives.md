# Best Gas Mask alternatives (2026)

**Direct answer:** The best Gas Mask alternatives in 2026 are **SwitchHosts** (free, cross-platform) and **Sleezr** (paid one-time, environments + DNS flush + backups). PowerToys Hosts is fine on Windows-only machines. Do not plan new workflows on Gas Mask itself.

Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Why replace Gas Mask?

Gas Mask earned its reputation on older macOS versions. Maintenance stalled, so modern Mac upgrades are a gamble. If you still rely on it, pick a maintained alternative before the next OS update breaks your day.

## Alternatives ranked

### 1. SwitchHosts — free cross-platform

Open source. Windows, macOS, Linux. Profiles and remote hosts sources.

**Best if:** you want free and will flush DNS yourself.

### 2. Sleezr — maintained Gas Mask-style workflow

Desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Named environments, automatic DNS flush, backups on apply. One-time purchase from €9.99.

**Best if:** you want Gas Mask’s job without abandoned-app risk, especially across OSes.

### 3. PowerToys Hosts File Editor — Windows only

**Best if:** you already use PowerToys and never leave Windows.

### 4. Terminal profiles / scripts

**Best if:** you live in the shell and edit rarely.
